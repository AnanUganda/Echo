Oct 21, 2026 · @Anan
Every page on echokenya.com is rewritten below, current text beside new, ready to paste. Stats and testimonials are kept as they are. Nine facts to confirm are listed at the end.
How the copy changed
• Real details over general claims. The homepage now names the trip length, dates, cost, and places (Nakuru, Maralal, Maasai Mara) that were only on the Itinerary page.
• AI-sounding phrases removed. Gone: "life-changing," "meaningful," "profound," "incredible," "journey," "more than a trip," stacked lists of three, and most em dashes.
• Plain headings. Slogans like "To Serve. To Connect. To Grow." become headings that say what's in the section.
• Kenyan partners lead. The copy says plainly that local pastors set the work and ECHO teams support it.
• Clearer buttons. "Explore Trips" and "View Upcoming Trips" become one consistent label: "See Upcoming Trips."
Homepage
The homepage keeps its structure; every heading and paragraph is rewritten to be concrete and plain. Testimonials and stats stay as given. Text in [brackets] needs a fact from you.
